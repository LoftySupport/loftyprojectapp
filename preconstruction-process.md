# Lofty preconstruction process

Transcribed from two files Amber supplied on 2026-08-16:
`Lofty_precon_CSV_import_system_sketch_test.xlsx` (the step list) and
`Lofty_process_mapp.csv` (the same process with its checklists and SLAs).

This is a **record of the business process**, not a description of the app. The app
does not model it yet — see [What the app has today](#what-the-app-has-today).

## Where preconstruction sits

The eight stages in `stages` are the whole process end to end. Preconstruction and
construction are two *phases* within it. Preconstruction runs from the PWA being
issued to `Released to Construction`, which spans the first five stages:

| # | Stage | Phase |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sales & acquisition | Preconstruction |
| 2 | Planning & Engineering | Preconstruction |
| 3 | Working Drawings & Contracts | Preconstruction |
| 4 | Preconstruction | Preconstruction |
| 5 | Scheduling & Estimating | Preconstruction |
| 6 | Construction & execution | Construction |
| 7 | Post-construction & closeout | not yet named |
| 8 | Handover & maintenance | not yet named |

The phase and the fourth stage share the name *Preconstruction*. That is Lofty's own
vocabulary and is left as-is here.

Stages 7 and 8 have not been assigned to a named phase by the business. The app groups
them as "Post-construction" so they are reachable, which is an assumption, not a fact.

## What the app has today

Two things exist that are in the neighbourhood of this process, and neither is it:

- **`stages`** — the eight above. A job sits in exactly one, and `jobs.stage` is the
  single answer to "where is this job". The board's columns are these.
- **`template_checkpoints`** — `stageId`, `label`, `position`: what a stage expects done
  before it hands over. The seeded data has **four invented placeholder checkpoints per
  stage**, written to give the UI something to render. They are not Lofty's steps.

So the 57 steps have no home yet. Mapping them onto the five preconstruction stages is a
business decision — which step belongs to which stage, whether a step can be skipped, and
whether the SLA days above should drive the same "days in stage" the board already shows
from `template_phases.expectedDays`. None of that is decided, and none of it is guessed
here.

What *is* decided, and is live in `app/src/data/savedViews.ts`, is the phase split in the
first table — the saved views on the Jobs and Projects boards read it.

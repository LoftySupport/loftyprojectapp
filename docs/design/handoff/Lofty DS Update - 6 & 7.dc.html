DESIGN SYSTEM UPDATE

Patterns from the job record and the sidebar

Eight patterns settled in the Lofty Hub mockups, proposed as additions to Lofty's App Design System. Each is shown light and dark. The drop-in files sit in ds-update/ — components, token additions, the traced icon set and a changelog entry.

01

NavRail

components/navigation/NavRail

The persistent dark application rail. One component with two widths and four states — 224px expanded, 64px collapsed, each with and without a flyout. Selection is a 20% white wash, hover 12%, rules 16%. A Foundation Black rail is the same material on both themes, so the rail itself does not change; the page around it does.

04

FieldRow

components/records/FieldRow

One row system for every record: label column 120px in the drawer and 136px on the page, value inline, 32px row. A field with a type shows its control, not the word "Empty" — an empty date is a date box reading dd/mm/yyyy. In a column of controls every control is the same width and height.

components/records/ProcessSteps

06

StageTrack & HealthChip

components/records/StageTrack

Status carries the health colour: the current stage takes the same fill as the health chip, so stage and health read as one signal. Completed stages are Foundation Black; not started are Flint 200. On the readouts the label is dark semibold and the value light — inverted from the usual field pairing, because the label is what you scan for.

RecordTabs, RecordBreadcrumb

components/navigation

Tabs need delineation when they sit on a record's docked panel: 36px, 16px padding, dividers between, the active one lifted as a white card with a Crisp Orange underline on a Flint 100 strip. The breadcrumb is three levels, and the job title carries its project number as a quiet link — marked with a light Flint underline, not colour.

Lofty construction glyphs, as SVG

assets/icons-lofty

Fourteen glyphs traced from the client's PNGs onto the 24×24 grid at one stroke weight, inheriting ink through currentColor. Approximations, not masters — replace them if vector originals arrive. Admin.png and AdminConsole.png were the same mark at two weights and are now one file. Full audit in ICONS.md; PNG-versus-SVG comparison in icon-proof.html.
